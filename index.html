Weapon Quality

Weapon quality is the part that directly increases your damage. Armor quality is survivability, not a direct DPS upgrade, so it is intentionally not counted here as damage progression.

Do this: Keep pushing weapon quality when the gold efficiency makes sense. Don’t recraft a weapon just to chase quality.
02
Essential
Item Level / Honing Distribution

More item level means more raw stats. For pure offense, weapon upgrades matter most, followed roughly by gloves, shoulders, helmet, pants and chest.

Do this: Prioritize weapon when you want damage, but keep armor at the raid’s expected level so you don’t get punished by defense gating.
03
High
T4 Accessory Effects

Your refined accessory lines are real DPS. The best line depends on class and build: crit rate, crit damage, outgoing damage, attack power and similar effects are not equal for everyone.

Do this: Compare the actual offensive lines for your exact build instead of buying an accessory only because its combat power looks higher.
04
Essential
Accessory Quality / Enlightenment Points

T4 accessory quality controls base stats and Ark Passive points. On Ancient accessories, 67+ quality is the clean target for full Enlightenment points.

Do this: First make sure your total Enlightenment points hit the breakpoint your build needs; only then min-max the main-stat value.
05
Essential
Card Set

Your equipped 6-card set directly changes damage. LOS 30 is the default baseline, while elemental sets can beat it when the boss is weak to that element and your awakening level is high enough.

Do this: Save raid-specific card presets and swap when an elemental weakness actually beats your normal set.
06
Min-max
Card Catalog / Book Bonuses

Completed books give roster-wide stats and bonus damage versus enemy types. Small individually, meaningful when stacked over a developed roster.

Do this: Finish efficient books that grant your combat stat or damage against the enemy types appearing in current raids.
07
Min-max
Legendary Skin Stats

Legendary skins provide more main stat than Epic skins. It is not a huge jump, but it is permanent character power.

Do this: Treat this as late-game optimization—not something to prioritize over core progression systems.
08
Medium
Roster / Horizontal Combat Stats

Roster progression and permanent stat potions add main stat and combat stats such as Crit, Specialization and Swiftness.

Do this: Complete the horizontal content that awards permanent combat-stat and skill-point potions. These are free, permanent upgrades.
09
High
Pet Stat & Pet Ability

Use the pet stat that matches your build, then optimize the pet combat ability such as Additional Damage or enemy-type damage.

Do this: Verify your pet is actually set to the correct combat stat. This is one of the easiest mistakes to fix.
10
Essential
Combat Level 70

Being below 70 costs damage, skill points, Ark Passive points and max mana. Level 70 is not optional for a finished endgame character.

Do this: Get the character to 70 before worrying about tiny late-game min-max gains.
11
Essential
Skill Points

Your main damage skills need enough points to reach the correct skill levels, often level 14 on the most important abilities.

Do this: Collect missing Skill Point Potions and make sure your core damage skills are not sitting below the build’s intended level.
12
Medium
Kurzan Contribution

Its combat bonuses apply in relevant Kazeros / Epic Raid content. At level 10, the offensive bonus includes Attack Power.

Do this: Level it if you are actively raiding the content where it applies; don’t count it as universal character-sheet DPS.
13
Seasonal
Paradise Orb

Paradise Orbs provide a small seasonal power gain tied to your Paradise progress and orb effect. The value varies by orb and your Paradise Power.

Do this: Use an offensive orb that fits your character and keep current-season Paradise progression up to date.
14
Essential
Ark Grid Cores

Order Cores are class-specific; Chaos Cores are universal. Core effects unlock at point thresholds, and Ancient cores can improve the 17-point effect.

Do this: Use the correct Order setup for your class and push the relevant 10 / 14 / 17 / 18 / 19 / 20 point breakpoints.
15
Essential
Astrogems

Astrogems both activate your Core thresholds and add direct offensive stats such as Damage, Additional Damage and Boss Attack.

Do this: Optimize Willpower efficiency first so the whole line activates, then chase useful DPS lines and enough Core Points for your target threshold.
16
Essential
Ark Passive Setup

Evolution, Enlightenment and Leap define the modern endgame build. This is also where much of the old Elixir/Transcendence power went: their set-style and DPS/support effects were folded into Evolution nodes, while basic stats were moved into gear.

Do this: Use a current post-removal class build and adjust Evolution for your actual crit, gems, party synergy and speed—not an old Elixir/Transcendence-era preset.
17
Essential
Karma

Karma upgrades Ark Passive-related power. Enlightenment levels add Weapon Power, Enlightenment ranks grant points, and Leap progression improves Hyper Awakening / Leap access.

Do this: Finish the meaningful ranks/levels for your build before spending heavily on tiny luxury upgrades elsewhere.
18
High
Ability Stone

Your stone still contributes through its two engraving effects and T4 stone bonuses. The automatic cutter makes the system easier, not irrelevant.

Do this: Cut the two engravings your final setup actually uses and stop when the next upgrade is worse gold-per-DPS than other systems.
19
Essential
Combat Engravings

Use the correct five combat engravings for your build. Your old class engraving is not a separate T4 engraving slot—it lives in Ark Passive Enlightenment.

Do this: Finish the five combat engravings and their book progression; only use cheaper conditional options when your build can satisfy them without losing uptime.
20
Essential
T4 Bracelet

T4 bracelets provide Leap Points plus fixed and rolled combat bonuses. The important part is not a generic Combat Power number—it is whether the bracelet gives the points and offensive effects your build actually benefits from.

Do this: Secure the Leap-point breakpoint your build needs, then compare the rolled offensive effects for your exact crit and damage profile.
21
Essential
Gems

Damage gems are direct multipliers; cooldown gems can change your entire rotation. The value depends heavily on which skills actually deal your damage.

Do this: Upgrade your highest damage-share skills first and hit cooldown breakpoints that create a cleaner, repeatable rotation.
22
Essential
Skills & Skill Tree Choices

Tripod/Skill Tree levels no longer exist. In PvE, the game automatically applies the former max-level effect. What still matters is choosing the correct Skill Tree options for your build and the encounter.

Do this: There is nothing to level or transfer anymore. Run the current core Skill Tree setup, then use raid presets for counter, push immunity, meter generation or utility swaps.
23
High
Skill Runes

Galewind, Wealth, Quick Recharge and other runes can be the difference between the intended rotation and a build that constantly desyncs.

Do this: Finish the missing runes your class guide actually calls for and put the strongest versions on the skills that benefit most.

Priority labels are intentionally broad: the exact gold-per-DPS order changes by class and by how complete your character already is.

Inside the raid

Things that raise real raid DPS

✦

Support uptime

Brand, attack buffs, identity buffs and offensive bracelet effects are enormous. A strong support can contribute a huge share of your effective damage.

◎

Party synergy

Crit synergy, positional synergy, attack/movement speed and mana support can materially change the best Ark Passive setup and your ceiling.

⚗

Food & Stronghold feast

Main stats, weapon power, mana recovery and attack/movement speed can fix resource or speed breakpoints and improve uptime.

◆

Battle items

Dark Grenades, Atropines and correct utility usage create real damage windows. Don’t overlap debuffs that do not stack.

↻

Fight-specific skill preset

Swapping a counter, meter generator, rune or defensive tripod can increase your ceiling if the encounter allows it.

⌁

Boss uptime

Knowing when the boss becomes untargetable, moves, turns or enters a mechanic lets you hold burst, pre-build resources and avoid wasting major cooldowns.

Beyond character progression

Other Factors

⚔

Class

Different classes and builds have different ceilings depending on balance, raid design and damage profile. This affects DPS, but it is not an upgrade you progress on your character.

🎲

Luck / RNG

Crit streaks, boss aggro, pattern selection and random movement can make an otherwise identical pull look significantly better or worse.

◉

Greed

Taking extra damage windows can raise your parse, but getting interrupted, knocked down or killed can cost far more DPS than the extra hit was worth.

▣

PC Specs / Settings

Frame drops, stutter, display settings and poor system performance can hurt execution and uptime even though they do not change your character's actual stats.

↯

Ping

Latency can affect skill timing, movement, animation responsiveness and tight action-combat windows. It matters for performance, but it is not an in-game progression system.

Special Mention
Esther / Sidereal Weapon

Esther weapons can provide additional power and unique Sidereal effects, but they are an exceptionally expensive, niche progression path. They are real DPS progression for the tiny number of players using them, not something the normal checklist should tell everyone to pursue.
